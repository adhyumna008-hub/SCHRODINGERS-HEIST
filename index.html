Miso’s way home

Recover three memory archives: Identity in sector 5, Destination in sector 10, and the Return Key in sector 13. Their exits stay locked until the archive is recovered. In sector 15, repair all three circuits, transfer inside, activate ISOLATE above the core, then PURGE below it. Escape to play the finale. Completed-game players can replay the ending from the toolbar.

Embedded synthetic voice-over accompanies the opening, sector briefings, debriefings and ending. V toggles narration; M mutes all sound. Subtitles remain visible. Movie controls: P pause, Space next shot, Esc skip.

Learn one move at a time

Calibration: stand on the upper plate, split, and move one cat to the lower plate. Both plates open the exit for five seconds. Gallery: leave a cat on the plate, reach the switch, then the exit. Switchyard: program X to disable lasers; enter X → X → H at the door. Receiver: collect the beacon, program X → H, approach the gap and throw across it.

Read the warning, then decide

Your active cat gets 0.5 seconds to escape a camera. The unattended cat gets 1.75 seconds. Tab to rescue it, R to recall it, or sacrifice it: the camera watches that spot for three seconds. You keep the cat you control. A measured active cat becomes your checkpoint.

Spend coherence deliberately

Split or deploy a beacon: −8/sec (never stacked). Merge and consume the beacon: +4/sec. Zero coherence collapses the branches and blocks recovery for three seconds. Finish with 40% for the efficiency star; avoid measurements for the stealth star. Par time and your saved ghost reward faster replays. Faint vent covers offer alternate routes while split.

Quantum field notes

X/H use state-vector matrix operations. Gate previews show amplitudes; probability is amplitude squared. Teleportation runs a three-qubit circuit with Bell measurement, two classical bits and X/Z correction. The facility closes an observed door with a classical safety interlock. Independent cats, selected survivors, vent travel, shared beacon damage and a coherence budget are gameplay abstractions.
